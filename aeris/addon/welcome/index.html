All set

Aeris is ready ✨

  • The Aeris button (the round one on the toolbar) opens your side panel: Customize, 📝 Notes, 🧩 Apps and 🗂️ Spaces. Alt+Shift+A works too.
  • Notes: right-click any text on a website → Clip to Aeris Notes.
  • Spaces: keep School, Games and Music tabs apart, and switch between them in the panel.
  • Split view: right-click a tab → Add split view to see two pages side by side.
  • Focus mode: Alt+Shift+F, or the ⛶ button in the address bar, hides the bars until you point at the top.
  • Something annoying on a website? Right-click it → Hide this on the website.
