Home screen

Put Aeris on your home screen

Aeris is already on your home screen. ✓

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) in Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Aeris from its globe icon. It opens full-screen, like an app.
  1. Tap Chrome's ⋮ menu.
  2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Want the full Aeris browser on Android, with tabs, themes on websites and an ad blocker? Ask for the Aeris app (.apk).

All set

Aeris is ready ✨

  • The ⋮ menu has 📝 Notes, 🗂️ Spaces, ⚡ Quick Commands, ⛶ Focus mode and page filters.
  • Notes: press and hold a link → Clip to Aeris Notes, or clip the whole page from the menu.
  • Spaces: keep School, Games and Music tabs apart. Switch at the top of the tabs list.
  • Split screen: press and hold a link → Open in split screen.
  • Something annoying on a website? ⋮ → Hide something on this page, then tap it.
  • ⚙ Settings on your start page changes the colour, theme, widgets and wallpaper.
  • Shortcuts: tap + in the dock for one-tap Discord, Spotify, WhatsApp and more.
  • Bubbles pop when you tap them. 🫧
